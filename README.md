# Site-Sense
---

**SiteSense** is an AI agent system that monitors construction sites for two categories of worker risk: PPE non-compliance and heat exhaustion. Using a multi-agent pipeline, it detects missing safety equipment and elevated body temperature signals, classifies findings by severity against real safety-code and occupational heat-stress thresholds, and routes alerts to site managers in a way that surfaces genuine risks without drowning them in noise — including a dedicated false-positive filtering layer for heat detection, since ambient sun exposure and camera drift can easily be mistaken for physiological heat stress. Every flagged incident is logged with a timestamped compliance trail, giving managers both real-time alerts and a historical record. Built with construction's climate conditions in mind — particularly relevant in high-heat regions of China where heat exhaustion is an underrepresented but serious site risk alongside traditional PPE compliance.
