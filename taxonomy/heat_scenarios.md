**Heat Scenarios**

## Baseline for everything is fine case

gradual rise in temperature in the morning, peak in early afternoon and slowly decline of temperature towards the evening 

##  Direct sun heat accumulation

A worker doing continuous labour under direct sun from morning onwards. No shade, less breaks. 

Shape: non-linear, concave up rise and when worker is taking a break a sharp drop is noticed

Labour intensity: Caution and High risk depending on the WBGT value

## Brief Spike from machinery, jumping, climbing stairs (should NOT trigger) (false positive testing)

A worker briefly walks/stands past/near a generator, or poured concrete to cure under the sun then moves on 

Shape: sharp spike and returns to baseline

Labour intensity: Normal (Level 1)

## Fatigue accumulated from work and then partial rest recovery

A worker continously worked for 3 cycles of 40 minute each with 10 minutes of water break. Each work cycle will increase the temperature, and each rest cycle will bring it down but not fully to the baseline

Shape: 3 rising segments with partial dip 

Labour intensity: Caution (level 2)

## Sustained Heat Impact

A worker continuously worked under direct sunlight without taking a break. As the heat increases, the WBGT gradally crosses the extreme threshold. The worker continues working until the system detect the sustained heat exposure. 

Shape: Gradual upward trend and reaches peak until the worker takes a break/moves to shade showing a smooth decline

The system should recognise this as a sustained heat risk event, and trigger warning to stop work and take a rest break. 

Labour intensity: High risk and extreme (level 3 and 4)
