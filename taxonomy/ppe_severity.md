# PPE Severity Levels


This taxonomy is based on Chinese occupational safety requirements for construction sites.



## Critical (level 1)

Violations that present an immediate risk of fatality or severe injury.

Examples

- No safety helmet
- No fall protection while working at height (>2 m)
- No boots worn
- No gloves and goggles worn 
- No safety harness worn while working at height 
- Entering restricted areas without PPE

Recommended Action

- Stop work immediately
- Notify site supervisor
- Correct PPE before continuing work

---

## Moderate (level 2)

Violations that increase injury risk but are not immediately life-threatening.

Examples

- Safety vest not worn
- Gloves missing during manual handling
- Eye protection missing during cutting or grinding
- Improper footwear

Recommended Action

- Correct before continuing work
- Supervisor notification

---

## Minor (level 3)

Administrative or low-risk PPE issues.

Examples

- Helmet chin strap unfastened
- Reflective vest partially covered
- Dirty or damaged PPE requiring replacement

Recommended Action

- Worker reminder
- Replace PPE if needed

**level 1** : No PPE 
**level 2** : Basic PPE (e.g., gloves, safety glasses)
**level 3** : Intermediate PPE (e.g., hard hat, steel-toed boots, high-visibility vest)
**level 4** : Advanced PPE (e.g., full-body harness, respirator)
