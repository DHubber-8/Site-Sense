# Heat Thresholds

## Purpose

This defines the heat stress thresholds used by China's construction safety measures

The thresholds are based on:

**《防暑降温措施管理办法》**（安监总安健〔2012〕89号） Section 1

**GBZ/T 229.3-2025 – Classification of Occupational Hazards in Hot Working Environments** Section 1

**Supplementary context:** High-Temperature Allowance in China: FIEs’ Compliance Checklist at https://www.china-briefing.com/news/china-high-temperature-allowance-compliance-guide/ Section 2

---

# Section 1 - Heat stress assesment using WBGT

These thresholds come from heat stress standards and this is what engineers use to account for air temperature, humidity, wind speed and etc. So the WBGT (Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature) is scientifically more accurate

## Risk Level: Normal (WBGT < 28°C )
Continue normal work and routine monitoring 

## Risk Level: Caution (WBGT: 28–30°C )
Increase hydration and more breaks 

## Risk Level: High Risk (WBGT: 30–32°C)
Reduce workload, increase rest frequency and monitor workers temperature closely

## Risk Level: Extreme (WBGT > 32°C)
Recommend suspension of heavy outdoor work and move workers to a shaded or cooler area

# Section 2 - Level Compliance 
This summarises the chinese labour regulations rather than the standards 

Daily maximum temperature (T) means the daily forecast issued by a meteorological station affiliated with a city-level-or-above meteorological authority.
Refresh this forecast input whenever the issuing station publishes a revised daily forecast; use the latest issued forecast for the alert check.

**Level 1 - High Temperature Alert**
Using the authoritative daily forecast input, 35°C ≤ T < 37°C

- Implement heatstroke prevention measures
- Provide sufficient drinking water
- Have more water breaks and rest 
- Overtime for outdoor activities should not be arranged

AI response:
- Increase hydration reminders
- Alert Site supervisor
- Increase monitoring frequency

**Level 2 - Severe Heat Alert**
Using the authoritative daily forecast input, 37°C ≤ T < 40°C

- Outdoor working hours must not exceed more than 6 hours per day
- Outdoor work shall not be arranged during the hottest 3 hours of the day
- Increase rest periods

AI response: 
- Alert site supervisor
- Record worker exposure to heat duration 
- Increase hydration breaks 

**Level 3 - Extreme heat Alert**
Using the authoritative daily forecast input, T ≥ 40°C

- Outdoor work should be suspended

AI response:
- Trigger Extreme Heat Alert (T ≥ 40°C)
- Immediate Suspension of outdoor work
- Alert Site supervisor immediately


PS: The AI system is a decision making tool and does not replace the judgement of a Qualified Person (QP).
